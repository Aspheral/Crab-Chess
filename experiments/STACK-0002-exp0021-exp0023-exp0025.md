# STACK-0002: EXP-0021 + EXP-0023 + EXP-0025

This is the renewed STACK-0002 interaction study requested after the earlier EXP-0021 + EXP-0023 stack failed to reproduce EXP-0023's standalone gain.

## Components

- **EXP-0021**: loose-alpha quiet-move LMR adjustment.
- **EXP-0023**: simplified non-PV razoring margin. This component is already part of accepted `main` and is therefore not applied a second time in the candidate patch.
- **EXP-0025**: penalize sufficiently deep inexact TT entries whose bound mismatches the current search window.

The candidate patch on this branch therefore adds only the EXP-0021 and EXP-0025 deltas to current accepted `main`, while the stack identity records all three intended components.

## Why retry EXP-0021?

The earlier STACK-0002 experiment combining EXP-0021 + EXP-0023 produced 39/430/43 over 512 games versus its accepted Crab control, scoring 49.6094% (approximately -2.71 Elo, draw-aware screening CI [-14.78,+9.34]). Its SF18 leg was positive at 53/422/37 (+10.86 Elo), but unresolved. The direct-Crab failure classified that interaction as an archive/unmerge decision.

This experiment asks a narrower follow-up question: **does EXP-0025's TT-window correction change the EXP-0021 + EXP-0023 interaction enough to recover a positive cumulative result?**

## Test policy

The first screen is 512 games versus accepted Crab and 512 versus untouched SF18 at Threads=1, Hash=64 MiB, 3+0.03, concurrency=2, sequential fixed 8-ply openings with repeated colors, and no adjudication.

A positive direct-Crab result is required before replication. If the renewed stack materially outperforms the previous EXP-0021 + EXP-0023 result and remains positive against Crab, replicate the exact stack and then perform component ablation. Standalone Elo estimates are never added together.

A positive SF18 screen is necessary context, but a 512-game positive result alone is not sufficient to declare Crab stronger than Stockfish 18.

## Crab v1 gate

`Crab v1` is reserved for the later cumulative candidate formed from the validated STACK-0002 result together with the accepted STACK-0001 lineage. Before v1 is declared official, that exact cumulative candidate must pass full correctness/provenance validation and a matched-resource strength test showing a statistically meaningful advantage over untouched Stockfish 18. No v1 claim is made by this experiment alone.
