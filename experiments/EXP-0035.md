# EXP-0035: Reduce quiet-history eval-difference update gain

## Classification

**REJECTED / NOT STACK-ELIGIBLE** after complete audited four-leg screen.

The candidate changed only the parent quiet-history update from `evalDiff * 9` to `evalDiff * 8`; the shared pawn-history multiplier remained `evalDiff * 13`.

The candidate was not promoted. It is not a clear catastrophic loss, but both direct comparisons against the accepted Crab baseline were directionally negative, with no positive signal to justify replication or inclusion in the experimental stack.

## Decision rationale

The direct Crab comparisons, which control promotion, are negative in both time controls. The 3+0.03 result is moderately negative and the fixed-budget result is effectively flat-to-negative. The positive 500 ms/move result against SF18 is mandatory context only and does not establish SF18 parity or superiority.

Because the candidate lacks a consistent positive direct-Crab signal, it is not stack-eligible and no replication or ablation is warranted. The complete evidence remains preserved in the workflow artifact; no results are fabricated or discarded.
