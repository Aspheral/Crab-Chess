# EXP-0035: reduce quiet-history eval-difference update gain

Status: draft experiment, not promoted.

## Hypothesis

Reduce the parent quiet-history update from `evalDiff * 9` to `evalDiff * 8`, leaving the shared pawn-history update at `evalDiff * 13`. The goal is to reduce noisy reinforcement from volatile static-evaluation differences while preserving the existing signal path.
