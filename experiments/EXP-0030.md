## Hypothesis / applicability
Accepted Crab still aborts Syzygy PV extension using wall-clock milliseconds even when `nodestime` is active, and its MultiPV extension budget is not divided across displayed PVs. The upstream correction is applicable to Crab.

The candidate keeps PV extension deterministic under nodestime because local `Position::do_move()` calls do not consume the global node budget. Under ordinary time management it scales the wall-clock abort threshold by MultiPV so multiple PV extensions cannot independently consume the same half-overhead allowance.
