## Scope and applicability
Accepted Crab used wall-clock milliseconds to abort Syzygy PV extension even under `nodestime`, and did not divide the ordinary time-management allowance across MultiPV lines. The candidate changed only `engine/src/search.cpp` to disable that wall-clock abort under nodestime, scale the ordinary abort threshold by MultiPV, perform the initial abort check before PV mutation, and pass MultiPV into the extension helper.

GPLv3, inherited Stockfish copyright/upstream attribution, Crab executable/UCI identity, NNUE, website, and unrelated engine code were preserved.
