# EXP-0028 — integer move-horizon arithmetic

## Status

REJECTED — archived standalone evidence. Do not merge this candidate into accepted Crab main or the cumulative stack.

## Hypothesis

Current Crab still carries the centi-MTG arithmetic inherited from SF18-era time management. Replace it with integer move-to-go arithmetic, adapting official Stockfish commit `82908e0fd68a3a717e6152f66a297ecc97d9a6e9` to current Crab while preserving EXP-0027's explicit cyclic `movestogo` guard.

The upstream change passed large STC/LTC/sudden-death non-regression tests and removes fractional centi-MTG arithmetic. On Crab, the candidate was intentionally isolated so any effect from the coarser sub-second sudden-death horizon was measured rather than assumed neutral.

## Applicability check

Applicable with adaptation. Accepted Crab still used `centiMTG`, including the same time-left and cyclic-control scaling structure targeted by the upstream change. Crab additionally contains the promoted EXP-0027 condition `limits.movestogo == 0`; EXP-0028 preserved that condition rather than restoring the older upstream behavior.

The candidate changed only `engine/src/timeman.cpp` engine semantics. Existing GPLv3 text, Stockfish copyright/upstream attribution, Crab namespace, executable identity, UCI identity, build naming, network files, and website branding were untouched.

## Candidate diff

- `centiMTG` becomes integer `mtg` capped at 50 moves.
- Sub-second sudden-death reduction becomes `int(scaledTime * 0.05)` and remains disabled when explicit `movestogo` is present.
- `timeLeft`, cyclic `optScale`, and `maxScale` use direct integer-move arithmetic.

## Final classification

**REJECTED.**

The mandatory direct comparison against the latest accepted Crab baseline produced a materially negative point estimate (-11.54 Elo) with 17 more losses than wins. Although the confidence interval narrowly crosses zero, that is not interpreted as proof of no effect; rather, the observed signal is unfavorable and not promising enough to justify replication or stack admission. The small positive point estimate against immutable SF18 is unresolved and cannot override the direct latest-Crab control.

The change has no identified complementary mechanism strong enough to justify banking a negative standalone screen. EXP-0028 therefore stays out of the cumulative stack. The PR is archived unmerged so its full evidence remains auditable.
